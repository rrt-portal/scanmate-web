Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-15 · Product version 1.0.0

Overview

ScanMate helps you OCR screenshots and take smart actions (call, open links, maps). This policy covers the Android / iOS apps and the web preview at scanmate.apps.grok.bot and this GitHub Pages legal site.

Data we process

  • Images you choose — processed locally (on-device ML Kit / Vision or in-browser Tesseract). We do not operate a ScanMate upload server for your screenshots.
  • Scan history & settings — stored on your device (Room / SwiftData / IndexedDB / localStorage).
  • Purchases — handled by Google Play Billing / App Store / preview mock flags. We do not receive your full payment card data.
  • Ads (free tier) — Google AdMob (or demo stubs in the web preview) may process device advertising identifiers subject to Google’s policies and your consent / ATT choices.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal data. We do not require a ScanMate account. Screenshots are not sent to ScanMate-operated cloud OCR.

Children

ScanMate is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region).
